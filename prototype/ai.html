Dual Moving Average Crossover Strategy

A dual MA crossover strategy uses two moving averages of different periods — typically a short-term (e.g. 10-day) and a long-term (e.g. 50-day).

  • Buy signal: Short MA crosses above the long MA (golden cross)
  • Sell signal: Short MA crosses below the long MA (death cross)

Best conditions:

  • Trending markets with sustained directional moves
  • Lower volatility environments reduce whipsaw signals
  • Works well on daily or weekly timeframes
